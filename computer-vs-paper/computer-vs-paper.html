Urban green spaces

Urban parks and community gardens can improve quality of life in densely populated cities. Research suggests that accessible green spaces encourage physical activity and provide residents with places to meet.

However, the benefits are not distributed equally. In some neighbourhoods, new parks have been followed by rising rents, making it difficult for long-term residents to remain. Planners therefore need to consider access, affordability and community involvement together.

Some cities have responded by involving local groups in the design and maintenance of public spaces. This approach may help ensure that improvements reflect residents' needs and remain useful over time.
